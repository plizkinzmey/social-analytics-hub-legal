Privacy Policy

Effective / last updated: October 16, 2026

Social Analytics Hub ("the Service") is a privately operated integration for analyzing content and interacting with Instagram professional accounts explicitly authorized by their operator. The Service is under development; individual features described below are used only when enabled.

1. Information processed

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted through Meta, the Service may process: authorized Instagram professional account IDs, usernames and profile metadata; posts, Reels, captions, media identifiers and links; publication metrics; comments and replies (including text, comment ID, username, time, and relationship to a parent comment); and, when messaging features are enabled, incoming interaction events and records of permitted responses.

2. Purposes

Information is used to synchronize and analyze account content, count comments and keyword requests, generate analytics, respond to qualifying user interactions when enabled, maintain service reliability, prevent duplicate actions, and investigate errors. The Service does not sell personal information or use it for unrelated mass messaging.

3. Sources and access

Information is obtained through the Instagram API with the permissions granted to this application by an authorized professional account. Access to stored information and API credentials is restricted to the operator and services explicitly configured to support these tasks. The Service is not offered for public self-service sign-up.

4. Hosting and service providers

Operational data may be stored on privately operated infrastructure. Limited information may be processed by technical providers necessary to operate the Service, such as Meta's APIs and, if enabled, automation or AI analysis providers. This public legal website is hosted on GitHub Pages. No Instagram access tokens or comment databases are published on this site.

5. Retention and security

Data is kept only for as long as needed to operate the authorized integration, provide analytics, resolve operational issues, or comply with applicable obligations. Retention may depend on the type of record and the service configuration. The operator uses access controls and restricted credentials. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Choices and deletion

Authorized accounts can revoke the app's access through Instagram's connected apps settings. To request access to or deletion of personal information stored by the operator, contact the address below and identify the relevant Instagram account, comment or publication. Requests may require reasonable verification. For instructions, see Data Deletion Instructions.

7. Children and updates

The Service is not directed to children. This policy may be revised when features or processing practices change; the updated version and its date will be published here.

8. Contact

Data inquiries and deletion requests: directus1984@gmail.com.
